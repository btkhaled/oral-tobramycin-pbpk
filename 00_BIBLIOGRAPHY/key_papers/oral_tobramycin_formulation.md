# Oral Tobramycin Formulation Strategies — Literature Review

## 1. Current Status
- **No approved oral formulation** of tobramycin
- Currently administered: IV (systemic) and inhalation (pulmonary)
- Need for oral route: patient compliance, chronic therapy, resource-limited settings

## 2. Formulation Approaches

### 2.1 Self-Emulsifying Drug Delivery Systems (SEDDS/SMEDDS)

#### Asad et al. 2023 (PLoS ONE) — KEY PAPER
- **Strategy**: HIP + SEDDS
- **Counter-ions**: Sodium docusate (DOC), sodium dodecanoate (DOD)
- **Results**:
  - LogP improved 1500× (from -2.9 to ~0.6)
  - Stoichiometric ratio 1:5 optimal
  - SEDDS loaded at 1% w/v
  - Log D SEDDS/release medium >2
  - Dissociation 20% at intestinal pH within 4h
- **Limitation**: No in vivo data

#### Muhammad et al. 2022 (Lat Am J Pharm)
- Earlier SEDDS study for tobramycin
- Proof of concept

### 2.2 Nanoparticles

#### Hill et al. 2019 (J Funct Biomater) — PLGA NPs
- **Strategy**: PLGA + AOT complex
- **Size**: 178-267 nm
- **Loading**: 2.7-4.5 µg/mg
- **Activity**: MIC maintained at 1.25 µg/mL
- **Release**: Sustained over hours

#### Blanco-Cabra et al. 2022 (NPJ Biofilms) — KuDa NPs
- **Strategy**: Dextran SCPNs
- **Size**: 10-20 nm
- **Loading**: Up to 40 wt%
- **Advantage**: Charge neutralization → biofilm penetration
- **Focus**: Pulmonary, but principles apply

#### Khaled et al. 2026 (Molecules) — NCAs
- **Strategy**: Nano-sized colloidal assemblies
- **Focus**: Oral transition
- **Result**: Enhanced lipophilicity + antimicrobial activity

### 2.3 Permeation Enhancers

#### Bohley et al. 2024 (Adv Sci) — Review
- **C10 (sodium caprate)**: GRAS, clinical data, paracellular
- **SNAC**: FDA-approved for semaglutide
- **PPZ (1-phenylpiperazine)**: Novel, 30-day safety in mice
- **SDC (sodium deoxycholate)**: Bile salt, membrane fluidization
- **Safety**: No permanent permeability increase after 30 days

### 2.4 Other Approaches

#### Chitosan Nanoparticles
- Mucoadhesion
- Tight junction opening
- Paracellular permeation

#### Cyclodextrin Complexation
- Solubilization (not needed for tobramycin)
- Could improve stability

#### Liposomes
- Mostly pulmonary focus
- Oral potential for lymphatic transport

## 4. Knowledge Gaps

1. **No in vivo PK data** for any oral tobramycin formulation
2. **No PBPK modeling** of oral absorption with formulation parameters
3. **No clinical trials** of oral tobramycin
4. **Optimal strategy unknown** — multiple approaches untested head-to-head
5. **Food effect** not characterized
