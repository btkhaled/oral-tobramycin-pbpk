# Literature Search Strategy

## Research Question

**"Oral Tobramycin: From BCS IV to a Viable Oral Formulation"**

### PICO Framework
- **Population**: Patients requiring tobramycin therapy (CF, Gram-negative infections)
- **Intervention**: Oral tobramycin formulations (SEDDS, nanoparticles, HIP, permeation enhancers)
- **Comparison**: IV tobramycin (reference), inhalation tobramycin
- **Outcome**: Bioavailability, PK parameters, efficacy targets, safety

## Combined Search Strings

### Primary Search (Tobramycin + BCS + Oral)
```
("tobramycin" OR "tobramycine") AND 
("BCS" OR "biopharmaceutics classification" OR "oral bioavailability") AND
("oral" OR "formulation" OR "delivery" OR "absorption")
```

### Secondary Search (Tobramycin + Formulation)
```
("tobramycin") AND 
("SEDDS" OR "SMEDDS" OR "nanoparticle" OR "liposome" OR 
"hydrophobic ion pairing" OR "permeation enhancer" OR "chitosan")
```

### Tertiary Search (PBPK + Aminoglycosides)
```
("PBPK" OR "physiologically based pharmacokinetic" OR "PK-Sim" OR "ospsuite") AND
("aminoglycoside" OR "tobramycin" OR "gentamicin" OR "amikacin")
```

## Inclusion Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Language | English, French |
| Publication type | Peer-reviewed articles, reviews, book chapters |
| Date range | 1995-2026 (BCS foundation to present) |
| Study type | In vitro, in silico, in vivo, clinical, modeling |
| Relevance | Directly related to tobramycin OR BCS III/IV oral delivery |

## Exclusion Criteria

- Non-English/non-French publications
- Conference abstracts only (without full text)
- Single case reports
- Topical/inhalation-only studies without PK data
- Studies on other aminoglycosides without relevance to tobramycin
